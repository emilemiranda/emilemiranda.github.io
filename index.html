About EM

I’m EM, a geospatial professional focused on GIS development, spatial data workflows, analytics, cartography, and interactive web mapping. My work combines technical implementation with clear visual communication—organizing spatial data, building reproducible workflows, and presenting results through maps and web-based tools.

My portfolio brings together selected GIS projects, a live interactive map, and a focused professional resume. Together, they show how I approach geospatial problems from data preparation and analysis through visualization, documentation, and delivery.
